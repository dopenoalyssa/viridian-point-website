## Brand implementation

- Headings and CTA labels: Sora SemiBold.
- Heading emphasis: Libre Baskerville SemiBold Italic.
- Body and section labels: Montserrat, with uppercase section labels.
- Dark teal: #012E33; light text/surfaces: #F3F5F4.
- Brand gradient: #15C27E, #64D558, #D2EF23.
- Fonts are self-hosted in assets/fonts with their OFL licenses.
- Logo artwork is exported directly from the supplied Figma brand kit.
- Favicon variants use the exact exported logo inside a teal background.
- Brand colors remain fixed for normal device dark-mode preferences.

Figma source:
https://www.figma.com/design/zYSxtvSsnE1INFBWc258P3/Viridian-Point-s-Brand-Assets?node-id=0-1

## Readability and interaction update

- Larger body text across services, FAQs, process, timelines, pricing, and footer.
- Tighter Libre Baskerville tracking: -0.04em for emphasis and -0.025em for quotes.
- Original logo artwork on hover and keyboard focus, without added backgrounds,
  cards, or borders. Uploaded image files are unchanged, including white artwork.
- Full-width email galleries with 8px phone fades and 14px larger-screen fades.
- Pixel-sized gallery loops with extra hidden continuations on wider screens.
- Anchor offsets follow actual header height; menus switch at 1100px.
- Offscreen marquee motion pauses, and preview images prepare animation layers
  only while hovered on devices with a precise pointer.
